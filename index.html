About

I'm a Cloud, AI & Mobile Solutions Architect with 19+ years in enterprise architecture, designing secure, scalable systems for automotive, manufacturing, retail and logistics businesses across APAC, India and Europe. Today I'm a Technical Architect at Continental Tire in Malaysia.

My work sits where three things meet: Microsoft Azure (with Google Cloud and Firebase where they fit), applied AI — Azure AI, Microsoft Foundry, AI agents, GitHub Copilot and Claude — and cross-platform mobile with Flutter and .NET MAUI. I build in C# and Python, and I care most about the parts that decide whether a system survives production: identity, offline sync, cost and operability.

I lead multi-disciplinary teams across regions — setting architecture roadmaps, running modernisation programmes and establishing technical governance. Along the way I've architected 15+ enterprise mobile apps and cut cloud spend by around 20% through FinOps practice.

Outside work I run MSDEVBUILD: blog.msdevbuild.com for architect-level articles with real code and real trade-offs, azure.msdevbuild.com — a free, open-source Azure certification learning platform — and the MSDEVBUILD YouTube channel. Together with the original msdevbuild.com that's 280 published articles, 5M+ content views and 50+ conference talks. I'm a Microsoft Azure Content Hero, Microsoft Certified Trainer and C# Corner MVP.

Helping Students Worldwide Build a Strong Foundation for Their Future

Since 2007, I've dedicated my free time to mentoring students from underprivileged backgrounds — helping them find their path in technology, build careers, and transform their lives and their families' futures. Not for money. Just for the love of giving back.

❤️ Read My Full Story 🤝 Get Mentorship
My Story

I grew up in a poor family in India. My parents sacrificed everything to send me to school. When I graduated in 2007 from Coimbatore, I struggled to find work — and when I finally did, the pay barely covered food and shelter.

That experience shaped me. I realised that having a job — any job — was a foundation to build on. And I wanted to help other students find that foundation before they lost hope.

Since then, I've supported over 15,000+ students, been featured in newspapers for this work, and watched many of them go on to build successful careers and happy lives. I measure time not in months, but in the number of students I've seen succeed.

"I've dedicated my time to their careers, not money. Witnessing many lives change and families find happiness."
— Suthahar Jegatheesan
A Promise of Hope

If your parents are poor, it doesn't mean you have to be poor too. Start dreaming and working towards achieving the life you've imagined. Achievement is not the end — my sharing will continue as long as I'm here.
